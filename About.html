About NBFIRA

The Non-Bank Financial Institution Regulatory Authority supervises and regulates non-bank financial institutions in Botswana to ensure transparency, fairness, and consumer protection.

Who We Are

NBFIRA is responsible for supervising non-bank financial institutions and ensuring that they operate responsibly and ethically within Botswana.

The authority promotes public confidence in the financial sector through effective regulation, transparency, and consumer education.

Our History

NBFIRA was established to strengthen the supervision and regulation of non-bank financial institutions in Botswana and improve the stability of the financial sector.

Vision

To create a trusted and transparent financial environment that protects consumers and supports economic growth.

Mission

To regulate and supervise non-bank financial institutions through effective compliance, consumer protection, and public awareness.

Organizational Structure

Board of Directors

Provides governance and strategic direction.

Executive Management

Oversees operational and regulatory activities.

Compliance Department

Monitors institutional compliance and regulations.

Regulatory Mandate

  • Regulate non-bank financial institutions
  • Protect financial consumers
  • Promote transparency and accountability
  • Ensure compliance with financial laws
  • Support financial sector stability
